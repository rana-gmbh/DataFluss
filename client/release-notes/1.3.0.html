DataFluss 1.3.0

  • New: Films & Series. A database for the films and series you watched and want to watch. Search The Movie Database (TMDB) and take a title over with story, cast, poster and pictures. Rate it with up to five stars in half steps and note when and how you watched it.
  • Actors and directors get their own records, with the titles they are in and how many of them you have seen.
  • Bring your MyMovies collection along: an XML export from MyMovies is taken over with ratings and viewing dates.
  • Gallery and header: tables with a picture can be browsed as a poster gallery, and record pages show a large header.
  • New field type Rating (stars) for all your own tables.
  • New database: start empty, as Schulverwaltung, or from Films & Series, Library, Events or Inventory.
  • Remember sign-in: the app can keep a database's sign-in in the Keychain and sign in by itself.
  • Update the server to 1.3.0 first: this version changes the database.
