DataFluss 1.4.0

  • New: template catalog. Browse and install databases others shared on templates.datafluss.de, under "Add template" and in "New database". DataFluss checks every file itself before applying it and tells you when a newer version appears.
  • Templates from a file, and your own tables exported as a template to share or to use in another database.
  • New: Publish. Turn chosen tables into a read-only website with gallery, record pages and search, as a ZIP, in a folder, on your DataFluss server under /p/…, or on WebDAV or FTP. Only chosen fields that the publication's role may see go out; secret fields, history, people and devices never do.
  • Publish again, only the changes. "Neu veröffentlichen" sends only what changed, by hand, after changes or daily. If the role comes to see more than you confirmed, it waits for your confirmation.
  • From a connected Mac: see your server's publications and publish them again; your changes are synced first.
  • More targets: SFTP, S3-compatible storage, Netlify and GitHub Pages, and an optional password for sites on your server. Websites on databases.datafluss.de for accounts a reviewer unlocked.
  • Partial stars in the gallery, and security fixes for record pages, list headers and printouts.
  • Update the server to 1.4.0 first: this version changes the database.
